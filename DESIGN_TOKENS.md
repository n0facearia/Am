## Type Scale
Standard pixel-based scale for consistent hierarchy:
- `xs`: 12px
- `sm`: 14px
- `base`: 16px
- `md`: 20px
- `lg`: 24px
- `xl`: 32px
- `2xl`: 48px

## Mascot States
- **Idle**: Attentive and ready; subtle breathing animation to indicate presence.
- **Thinking**: Focused and processing; concentrated expression or rhythmic movement.
- **Success**: Cheerful and complete; a brief, positive burst of energy or a wink.
- **Error**: Concerned and alert; a slight tilt or a visual cue of "something is wrong."

## Transition Rules
- **State Changes**: 150ms (small, purposeful updates like hover or toggle).
- **Mode Switches**: 250ms (significant layout or color shifts between primary agents).
